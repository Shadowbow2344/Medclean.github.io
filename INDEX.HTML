Enhancing Medical Waste Sustainability

Hospitals have embraced sustainability through energy-efficient systems and reusable medical supplies, yet healthcare facilities across the UAE still generate around 21.5 tons of medical waste daily, with Abu Dhabi alone contributing approximately 12 tons per day. Much of this waste, including single-use gloves, masks, and PPE, remains non-biodegradable and ends up in landfills, posing significant environmental and health risks.

Our project builds on existing initiatives by exploring innovative solutions to reduce, reuse, and better manage hospital medical waste. Through this, we aim to enhance sustainability in healthcare while protecting both patients and our planet.

Our Solutions

Biodegradable Gloves

Our innovative biodegradable nitrile gloves decompose within 1-5 years instead of 100+ years, while meeting all medical safety standards (EN 455, EN 374). These gloves contain organic additives that enable 81-90% biodegradation in landfill conditions (ASTM D5526).

The Problem

Eco Bricks

Our innovative eco bricks transform non-hazardous medical plastic waste into durable construction materials. By sterilizing and compressing medical packaging waste, we create sustainable building blocks that help reduce landfill waste while providing affordable housing solutions.
